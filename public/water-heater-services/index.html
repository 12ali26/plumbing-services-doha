We do not diagnose remotely, but these details help us bring the right parts and set expectations.

Safety first

Hot water and electricity together carry a scald and shock risk. Do not open the heater. Switch it off at the board, and if it is discharging hot water, keep people away from the outlet.

FAQs

Questions about water heater services in doha

Is it worth repairing an old water heater?
If the tank itself is leaking, replacement is usually the sensible option. Element, thermostat and valve faults are normally repairable.
Water sprays from a valve on the heater — is that normal?
A little discharge when heating can be normal; a steady flow is not. It often points to a faulty pressure-relief valve or high incoming pressure. Have it checked.
Can you supply and fit a new heater the same day?
Often, depending on the size and type in stock. Send a photo of the current unit so we can match it.
