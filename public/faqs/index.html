Do you cover emergencies?
Yes. Our stated cover is 24/7 emergency response for urgent plumbing and electrical problems in Doha. Call the main number for the fastest response.
Which areas of Doha do you serve?
We work with homes and businesses across Doha and surrounding areas, Qatar. Share your neighbourhood when you contact us and we will confirm timing.
How do I get a price?
Send a description and photos by WhatsApp or use the callback form. Where possible we give an indication before the visit and confirm the cost on site before starting work.
Do you handle both plumbing and electrical work?
Yes — plumbing, electrical, water pumps, water tanks and water heaters, plus general building maintenance. Larger installations are quoted after a site assessment.
Can you help property managers with recurring maintenance?
Yes. See commercial maintenance for planned inspections and repairs across multiple units or sites.
What should I send when I first contact you?
Your area in Doha, a short description of the problem, one or two photos, and whether it is urgent. That is usually enough for us to advise the next step.
Do you charge a call-out fee?
Any call-out or after-hours charge is confirmed with you before a technician is dispatched. The owner should confirm the current policy before launch.
Can you give a fixed price over the phone?
For straightforward jobs we can give an indication. Anything involving hidden pipework, boards or pumps is confirmed on site before work starts.
Do you guarantee your work?
Workmanship and any warranty terms will be stated here once confirmed by the owner. Ask the technician what applies to your job.
Do you work on weekends and public holidays?
Our stated cover is 24/7 emergency response. Call the main number at any time for urgent problems.
Do you supply parts and materials?
Yes, or we can fit items you have already purchased. Tell us the model so we bring the right fittings.
Are you licensed and insured?
Any licences, registrations or insurance the business holds will be listed here once the owner supplies the details. We do not publish unverified credentials.
