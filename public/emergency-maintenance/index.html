If there is immediate danger

For fire, electric shock, flooding that threatens safety, or a gas smell, contact Qatar's emergency services first. Then call us so we can make the property safe and carry out repairs.

Emergencies we are called for

  • Burst pipes and major leaks
  • Ceiling leaks between apartments
  • No water to the property
  • Overflowing or backed-up drainage
  • Total loss of power or a circuit that will not reset
  • Burning smell or sparking from a fitting or board
  • Water heater leaking or discharging hot water
  • Booster pump failure leaving a building without supply

While you wait for us

  1. 1

    Make it safe

    Isolate the water at the main valve, or switch off the affected circuit at the distribution board — only if you can do so safely.

  2. 2

    Contain the damage

    Move valuables and electricals clear of water. Put down towels or a container under a drip.

  3. 3

    Call, then message

    Call the main number first. Then send your area and photos on WhatsApp so we arrive prepared.

  4. 4

    Keep the line open

    Stay reachable so the technician can call you for access and directions.

FAQs

Emergency questions

What counts as an emergency?
Anything causing damage or making a home unsafe or unusable right now — flooding, no water, no power, a leaking heater, or a burning smell from electrics.
Is there a call-out fee?
Any call-out or after-hours charge is confirmed with you before we are dispatched. The owner should confirm the current policy before launch.
How quickly can someone come?
We prioritise by risk and location and give you a realistic time when you call. We do not publish a fixed response-time guarantee.
